Built Between Cases

Tools for anaesthesia and intensive care, built by clinicians, most of them with AI.

About this list

Anaesthetists, intensivists and the people they work with are building their own tools, most of them with AI and usually in the gaps between cases. These are the ones I have opened and checked, each with its maker and the AI they used where that is known.

How a tool gets in

  • A clinician, or a clinician in training, made it.
  • It opened and worked when I checked it, and the entry shows the date.
  • If the maker has said which AI they used, or the web address shows it, the entry says so.
  • Listing is not endorsement, and makers can ask for a change or for their tool to come off at any time.
